# Lab QC privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Lab QC is a Forge app for Jira and Confluence that tracks laboratory quality-control results with Westgard rules.

## What the app stores

- Quality-control records (analyte names, control levels, target values, measured results and dates) and the app's settings, kept in Forge app storage inside your Atlassian site.
- Jira issues and Confluence pages that the app creates when you ask it to, kept in your own Jira and Confluence like any other issue or page.

The app does not collect or store personal data about users beyond what Jira and Confluence record themselves (for example, who created an issue).

## Where the data goes

Nowhere outside Atlassian. Lab QC runs on Atlassian Forge and makes no calls to outside servers. GreYat Labs cannot see your data.

## Removing data

Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage. Issues and pages it created stay in your site until you delete them.
